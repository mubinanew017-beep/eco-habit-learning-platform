
# 🌱 Eco Habits — Gamified Eco-Friendly Learning Platform

A web + offline learning platform that teaches Classes 1–9 students eco-friendly habits through games, quizzes, and real-life challenges — built for [Hackathon Name].

**🔗 Live Prototype:** https://eco-habits-education-pd0a.bolt.host/

---

## The Problem

- Students often find learning eco-friendly habits boring.
- Theoretical teaching doesn't grab attention or encourage action.
- Many students don't clearly understand why eco-friendly habits matter for themselves and the environment.

## Our Solution

A web app and offline card games that make learning eco-friendly habits fun and effective for students of Classes 1–9.

- **Classes 1–3:** offline card-based games (since web apps aren't suitable for very young children) — simple, hands-on games like *Eco Habits Uno* and segregation cards, purchasable via our website shop.
- **Classes 3–9:** interactive web games, quizzes, and real-life challenges to build eco-positive habits.

The app is easy to use, while keeping learning engaging and age-appropriate.

---

## Features

- 🎮 **Activity/Game-based learning** (digital or offline depending on age group)
- 🧠 **Quizzes** after each game to reinforce habits and test knowledge gained
- 🌍 **Real-life challenges** with photo-proof submission
- 🏆 **Leaderboard** for healthy competition
- ⭐ **Points and badges** to keep students motivated and proud

---

## Web App Games

**Classes 3–6**
1. **Water Conservation** — drag actions into "Do" or "Don't" bins (e.g. turning off the tap vs. leaving it running)
2. **Segregate Waste** — sort household waste (banana peel, plastic bottle, newspaper) into wet, dry, or recyclable bins
3. **Eco-Friendly Choices** — drag product images (cloth bag, plastic bottle, reusable cup) into "Good" or "Bad" for the environment

**Classes 7–9**
1. **Carbon Footprint Challenge** — estimate the carbon footprint of daily activities and explore eco-friendly alternatives
2. **Eco-Friendly Product Sorting** — sort items into biodegradable, recyclable, reusable, and harmful, then reflect on choices
3. **Energy Choices Simulator** — choose between solar, wind, fossil fuels, etc., weighing environmental impact and cost

## Offline Games (Classes 1–3)

Since children in Classes 1–3 are too young for apps, we designed physical card games for easy engagement:

- **Waste Sorting Cards** — kids drag/place picture cards into the correct bins (wet, dry)
- **Good or Bad Habit Sorting Cards** — sort habits (throwing trash on ground, watering plants, wasting water) into "Good" or "Bad" piles
- **Eco Uno** — like regular Uno, but cards show eco actions (e.g. "Use a cloth bag," "Plant a tree"); special cards skip turns or reverse play while reinforcing good habits
- **Carbon Footprint Game** — cards show daily activities with pollution points (e.g. "Car ride"); play low-pollution cards to win, use special cards to cancel high-pollution moves

These offline games are also available to buy from our website shop.

## Quizzes & Challenges

- Interactive quiz after each game to test knowledge gained
- Points, badges, and achievement tracking to keep interest high

**Real-life challenges** include planting a tree, meat-free meal days, and no-plastic days. To keep these genuine:
- Students upload photos with date/time as proof
- Teachers approve submissions and monitor progress
- This prevents cheating and ensures real eco-friendly behaviour

---

## Leaderboard

- Shows top-performing students in class or school
- Encourages fair and friendly competition
- Motivates students to stay consistent

---

## App Structure

- **Landing/Home Page** — banner, feature highlights, footer with shop/FAQ links
- **Student Dashboard** — sidebar nav, profile panel (name, class, points, badges), ongoing challenges, leaderboard snapshot
- **Games & Activities** — tabbed by class group (1–3 offline, 3–6, 7–9), each game card with instructions
- **Quiz Module** — progress bar, multiple question types, immediate feedback, badge pop-up
- **Challenges Page** — challenge list, photo upload with timestamp, teacher verification status
- **Leaderboard** — table/card view, filterable by class
- **Teacher/Admin Dashboard** — verification queue, approval tools, analytics on participation and points
- **Profile & Settings** — edit info, points/badge history, notification settings
